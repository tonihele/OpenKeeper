# Native carrier spread, 2026-10-30

Launched the original installation with `start-dkii.sh -level GemCarrierProbe_
-nomovies`, using the isolated map generated by `create_probe.py`. No debugger
was attached during this observation. The original create/attach trigger gives
the Lord the portal gem; the simulation then runs normally.

Recorded 20 seconds at 15 fps from the 800×600 game viewport:
`/tmp/dkii-gem-spread.mp4`. Representative walking frame:
`/tmp/dkii-gem-walking.png`; overview: `/tmp/dkii-gem-spread-sheet.png`.

Visible behavior:

- While stationary, overlapping sparkles form a compact, saturated white mass
  around the upper body, with pale yellow points around its edge.
- While walking, old particles remain behind and form a broad, irregular trail.
  The trail in the representative frame is roughly one projected body length;
  this is a visual comparison, not a measurement in world units.
- Individual pointed/star-shaped silhouettes are visible around the edges.
  Sharp rays therefore also occur in the original game, although overlap hides
  much of their shape in the bright center.

The camera zoom and position change during the recording. These images confirm
the shape and moving trail, but do not establish a numerical sprite-size ratio
against the close-up OpenKeeper ModelViewer camera. They also do not settle the
native generation-scale or update-cadence questions.
